Who we serve

Built for the operations where supervisors run the shift.

ACES works with sites where frontline leaders carry both the floor and the paperwork, and where leadership wants AI to reach the floor without burying the people who run it.

The operations

ACES works with manufacturing, warehousing, distribution and fulfillment operations whose supervisors run shifts of hourly employees, typically 100 to 5,000 people across one or more sites. Engagements are delivered nationwide, on site or remote.

The sponsor

The program is typically sponsored by a plant or site manager, a director of operations, or the leader responsible for training and development. It is designed for leadership teams that have been asked to bring AI into the operation and want their frontline leaders prepared before it reaches the floor.

The first task

Most sites start with incident and near-miss write-ups or end-of-shift reports. Every supervisor writes them, they follow a set format, and the time they take is easy to measure, which makes them the clearest place to prove the method before it spreads.

Start with a twenty-minute conversation.

Tell us which report or record is eating your supervisors' shifts, and we will tell you plainly whether the Pilot is a fit. There is no obligation.
