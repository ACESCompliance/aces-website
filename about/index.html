About

Nour Alsharife, Founder

Thirteen years in operations, safety and training, from supervising a floor to leading training and compliance programs across a region.

I have spent thirteen years in operations, safety and training across multi-site industrial environments, in roles that ranged from supervising a floor to leading training and compliance programs for supervisors and trainers across a region.

The Frontline AI Readiness Program began with a problem on my own team. Supervisors were losing their evenings to investigation write-ups. I built an AI tool that let a supervisor answer a short set of questions and receive a complete first draft for review. Hours of typing became minutes of review, and the recovered time went into prevention. Measurably fewer injuries followed.

That experience is the basis of the program. The documentation moves to the tool, the judgment stays with the supervisor, and the person who taught the method to the floor remains on the floor after the engagement ends.

AI that gives supervisors their time back, not their jobs. Nour Alsharife, Founder, ACES
