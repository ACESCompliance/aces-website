The problem

The best supervisor on your floor is doing paperwork.

Every plant has the same four tasks eating the same hours. None of them need a supervisor's judgment. All of them get one anyway.

30–45 min / shift

Shift handoff

Notes get rewritten into a report nobody reads until something goes wrong. The next shift starts cold.

1–3 hrs each

Incident write-up

The supervisor who saw it spends the evening typing it, instead of fixing what caused it.

Hours / week

Training records

Who's certified, who's due, who moved lines. Tracked in three places, right in none of them.

Hours / week

Work orders & daily summaries

The same numbers retyped into the same template, every day, by someone paid to run a line.

How it works

The AI Adoption Sprint. Thirty days, one plant, measured.

No software to buy. We work with the tools you already pay for. The work is getting your people to use them, and proving it with numbers.

1

Half-day leadership session

Plant leadership picks the two or three admin tasks worth taking off supervisors first, agrees what "working" looks like, and sets the rules for data, confidentiality and what AI is not allowed to touch.

2

Supervisor train-the-trainer

Hands-on with real tasks from your floor: notes in, report out, in your format. Your lead supervisors learn it well enough to teach their shifts, so it doesn't leave when we do.

3

30-day usage scorecard

Who's using it, on which tasks, and how much time came back. Weekly check-ins to fix what's stuck. At day 30 you have a number, not a feeling.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do we have to buy new software?

No. The Sprint runs on tools most plants already license: Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or similar. If you have none of them, we'll pick one together on the first call. The cost of the tool is a rounding error next to supervisor hours.

Is this about replacing supervisors?

No, and we say that out loud on the floor on day one. The work targets the paperwork around the job, not the job. Supervisors who get an hour back spend it on the line, with their people. That's where incidents get prevented and output gets made.

What about confidential production or safety data?

The leadership session sets the rules before anyone types anything: which data stays out, which tools are approved, and how records are kept. Most plants run on enterprise versions of these tools where your data isn't used for training. We work inside whatever your IT policy allows.

Where do you work?

Based in Pennsylvania and Florida. On site anywhere in the US; the leadership session and train-the-trainer also run well remotely for multi-site operations.

What does the first call look like?

Twenty minutes. You describe one admin task your supervisors hate. I tell you whether it's a good first target and what it would take. No slides, no pitch. If it's not a fit, you'll know in twenty minutes.
