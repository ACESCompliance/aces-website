The program

The Frontline AI Readiness Program

A thirty-day engagement for one site and up to twenty-five supervisors and leads, delivered on the AI tools the organization already licenses. No software is purchased and nothing is built from scratch.

1

Leadership alignment

A half-day working session with site leadership to select the first tasks, define what success looks like in hours, and set the rules for data handling, approved tools and boundaries.

2

Task design

For each selected task, ACES builds the prompt, the output template in the site's existing format, a one-page reference card and a short instructional clip, using the site's own forms and records.

3

Train-the-trainer

Lead supervisors work the method on their own tasks until it is routine, then learn to teach it to their crews in a fifteen-minute huddle. The capability stays with the site.

4

Adoption and measurement

Lead supervisors roll the method to their shifts. ACES checks in weekly, resolves what is stuck, and maintains an adoption record showing who is using it, on which tasks, and the minutes recovered.

5

Leadership read-out

At day thirty, leadership receives a written report covering hours returned per task, adoption by shift, and the recommended next tasks.

Scope and pricing are set after the first conversation, based on the site, the number of supervisors and the tasks selected.

What changes

The work that moves from the supervisor to the tool.

Supervisors keep the judgment. The tool takes the typing. Rough notes or a spoken summary go in, and a finished document in the site's format comes out for the supervisor to review and approve.

  • End-of-shift and handoff reports
  • Incident and near-miss write-ups
  • Training records and certification tracking
  • Work orders and maintenance requests
  • Daily production and operations summaries
  • Standard operating procedures and work instructions
  • Audit checklists and corrective action follow-up
  • Shift scheduling notes and staffing requests

Across two shifts a day, these tasks commonly return five to eight hours a week to each lead. The program records the actual figure at your site.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the Frontline AI Readiness Program?

A thirty-day engagement for one manufacturing, warehousing, distribution or fulfillment site. Supervisors learn to put AI to work on the reports, records and write-ups that consume their shifts, lead supervisors are trained to carry the method to every crew, and adoption is measured for thirty days so leadership can see retention.

Does the program require new software?

No. The program is delivered on AI tools most organizations already license, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT or Claude. If the organization has none, one is selected together during leadership alignment.

Is the program intended to reduce supervisor headcount?

No. The program moves documentation work from the supervisor to the tool. The supervisor keeps the judgment, the review and the approval, and the recovered time returns to the floor and the crew.

How is confidential production and safety data handled?

Leadership alignment sets the rules before any task is designed, including which data stays out, which tools are approved, and how records are retained. Enterprise versions of the supported tools do not train on customer data, and ACES works within the organization's IT policy.

Is the program delivered on site?

Both. Leadership alignment and train-the-trainer can be delivered on site anywhere in the United States or remotely, and the thirty days of adoption support are delivered remotely with weekly check-ins.
