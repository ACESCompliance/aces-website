Frontline AI Readiness Program

Your frontline leaders are being asked to bring AI onto the floor. No one has prepared them to lead it.

ACES prepares them. Through the Frontline AI Readiness Program, supervisors learn to put AI to work on the reports, records and write-ups that consume their shifts. The readiness gap

Leadership is already reporting the problem.

Operations leaders are not short on AI tools. They are short on frontline leaders who are prepared to introduce those tools to hourly teams and keep them in use. A 2026 survey of manufacturing leaders by The Manufacturing Institute and PwC puts numbers to it.

54%

rate their frontline leaders' ability to lead AI-driven change as low or very low.

19%

of manufacturers offer any AI-related training for their frontline leaders.

72%

name employee resistance as a top expected barrier to bringing AI into the operation.

Source: The Manufacturing Institute and PwC, Frontline Leadership in Manufacturing AI Adoption, 2026

Those organizations are the ones ACES was built for. The supervisor is the point where AI either enters the operation or stalls, and the supervisor is the person conventional AI training overlooks.
